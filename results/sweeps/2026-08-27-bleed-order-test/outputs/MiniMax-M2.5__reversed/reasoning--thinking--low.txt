=== CONTENT ===
The trucks arrive together whenever the elapsed time \(t\) (in minutes) after 6:00 AM is a common multiple of the three return intervals:

\[
t \equiv 0 \pmod{12},\quad t \equiv 0 \pmod{18},\quad t \equiv 0 \pmod{30}.
\]

The times are the multiples of the least common multiple of 12, 18, and 30.

\[
\text{LCM}(12,18,30)=2^2\cdot 3^2\cdot5=180\text{ minutes}=3\text{ hours}.
\]

Thus the trucks meet every 3 hours.  

From 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM is \(12\times60=720\) minutes, so the meeting times are

\[
0,\;180,\;360,\;540,\;720\ \text{minutes after 6:00 AM},
\]

i.e., at 6:00 AM, 9:00 AM, 12:00 PM, 3:00 PM, and 6:00 PM.

There are **5** such moments.

**ANSWER: 5**
