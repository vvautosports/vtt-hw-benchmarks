• Speculative decoding uses a small draft model to propose multiple tokens that a larger target model verifies in a single batch, accepting proposals when correct and falling back to normal generation when rejected.

• N-gram and lookup-based drafts excel on repetitive code and structured text but underperform on open-ended prose where acceptance rates drop, sometimes slowing generation below baseline.
