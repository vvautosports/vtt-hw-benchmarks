All three trucks start together at 6:00 AM (t = 0 min). They will next all arrive together at the least common multiple of their return intervals:  

- Truck A: 12 min  
- Truck B: 18 min  
- Truck C: 30 min  

**LCM(12, 18, 30) = 180 min** (3 hours).

Starting at 6:00 AM, the common arrival times within the 12‑hour window (6:00 AM to 6:00 PM inclusive, i.e., 0 ≤ t ≤ 720 min) are:

- t = 0 min → 6:00 AM  
- t = 180 min → 9:00 AM  
- t = 360 min → 12:00 PM  
- t = 540 min → 3:00 PM  
- t = 720 min → 6:00 PM  

That gives **5** moments when all three trucks are at the warehouse simultaneously.

ANSWER: 5
