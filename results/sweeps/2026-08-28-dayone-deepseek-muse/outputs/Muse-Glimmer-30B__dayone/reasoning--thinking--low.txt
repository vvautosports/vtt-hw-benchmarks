=== CONTENT ===
The common arrival interval is the least common multiple of the three return periods.

* $12 = 2^{2}\cdot 3$
* $18 = 2\cdot 3^{2}$
* $30 = 2\cdot 3\cdot 5$

\[
\operatorname{lcm}(12,18,30)=2^{2}\cdot 3^{2}\cdot 5 = 180\text{ min}=3\text{ h}
\]

Hence after the 6:00 AM coincidence the three trucks are together again every
180 min.

$6{:}00\text{ AM}+k\cdot180\text{ min}\le 6{:}00\text{ PM}$

$0\le k\cdot180\le 12\text{ h}=720\text{ min}$

$k_{\max}= \left\lfloor 720/180\right\rfloor =4$

$k=0,1,2,3,4$ gives

6:00 AM, 9:00 AM, 12:00 PM, 3:00 PM, 6:00 PM

i.e. $ \lfloor720/180\rfloor+1 =5$ coincident arrivals in the closed interval,
four coincidences after the initial 6:00 AM arrival.
