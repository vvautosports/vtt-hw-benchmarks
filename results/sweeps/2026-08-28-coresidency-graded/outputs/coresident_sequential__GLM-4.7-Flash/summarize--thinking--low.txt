*   **Workload Economics:** The approach varies in efficiency; n-gram drafting is highly effective for repetitive text like code but can slow down open-ended prose, whereas Multi-Token Prediction heads offer stable acceptance rates.
*   **MoE Complexity:** Mixture-of-Experts models reduce the profitability of speculation on bandwidth-bound hardware because verifying multiple positions in a batch forces the system to pull more expert weights through memory.
*   **Server Parallelism:** Speculative decoding competes with slot-based parallelism for compute headroom, requiring system tuning that treats speculation settings, slot count, and sampling parameters as a joint configuration.
*   **Optimization Metrics:** Effective tuning should measure end-to-end tokens per second on representative tasks rather than relying solely on microbenchmarks to account for the interaction between speculation and slots.

**TL;DR**
Speculative decoding accelerates inference by verifying draft tokens but faces challenges with Mixture-of-Experts architectures and server slot configurations that require careful, joint tuning to remain profitable.
