I need to find how many times all three trucks arrive at the warehouse simultaneously between 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM.

First, let me establish the time frame:
- From 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM inclusive = 12 hours = 720 minutes

Next, I need to find when all three trucks arrive at the same time.

Since:
- Truck A returns every 12 minutes
- Truck B returns every 18 minutes  
- Truck C returns every 30 minutes

All three trucks will arrive together at the same time when the arrival times coincide. This happens at the Least Common Multiple (LCM) of their intervals.

Finding LCM(12, 18, 30):
- 12 = 2² × 3
- 18 = 2 × 3²
- 30 = 2 × 3 × 5

LCM = 2² × 3² × 5 = 4 × 9 × 5 = 180 minutes

So all three trucks arrive together every 180 minutes.

Now I need to count how many times this happens between 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM:

Starting time: 6:00 AM (0 minutes)
Ending time: 6:00 PM = 12 hours = 720 minutes

The arrival times when all three trucks are together are:
- 6:00 AM (0 minutes)
- 6:00 AM + 180 minutes = 9:00 AM
- 9:00 AM + 180 minutes = 12:00 PM
- 12:00 PM + 180 minutes = 3:00 PM
- 3:00 PM + 180 minutes = 6:00 PM

Let me verify these times are within the 720-minute window:
- 0 minutes = 6:00 AM ✓
- 180 minutes = 3 hours = 9:00 AM ✓
- 360 minutes = 6 hours = 12:00 PM ✓
- 540 minutes = 9 hours = 3:00 PM ✓
- 720 minutes = 12 hours = 6:00 PM ✓

All five times are within the given period.

Let me double-check by seeing if there could be a sixth occurrence:
- Next occurrence would be at 720 + 180 = 900 minutes
- 900 minutes = 15 hours, which is after 6:00 PM
- So this is outside our time frame

Therefore, there are 5 times when all three trucks arrive simultaneously.
