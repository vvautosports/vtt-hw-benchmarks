I need to find how many times all three trucks arrive at the warehouse at the same time between 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM.

First, let me establish the time frame:
- From 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM is 12 hours
- This is 12 × 60 = 720 minutes

Next, I need to find when all three trucks arrive simultaneously.

Truck A returns every 12 minutes
Truck B returns every 18 minutes  
Truck C returns every 30 minutes

For all three trucks to arrive at the same time, I need to find the Least Common Multiple (LCM) of their return intervals.

Finding LCM of 12, 18, and 30:
- 12 = 2² × 3
- 18 = 2 × 3²
- 30 = 2 × 3 × 5

LCM = 2² × 3² × 5 = 4 × 9 × 5 = 180 minutes

So all three trucks arrive together every 180 minutes.

Now I need to count how many times this happens between 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM.

Starting at 6:00 AM, the arrival times will be:
- 6:00 AM (start time)
- 6:00 AM + 180 minutes = 9:00 AM
- 9:00 AM + 180 minutes = 12:00 PM
- 12:00 PM + 180 minutes = 3:00 PM
- 3:00 PM + 180 minutes = 6:00 PM

Let me verify these times are within the 12-hour window:
- 6:00 AM (inclusive)
- 9:00 AM (inclusive)
- 12:00 PM (inclusive)
- 3:00 PM (inclusive)
- 6:00 PM (inclusive)

All 5 times are within the given period.

I can also verify this by checking that 720 ÷ 180 = 4, which means there are 4 intervals of 180 minutes within the 720-minute window, plus the initial time, giving us 5 total times.

ANSWER: 5
