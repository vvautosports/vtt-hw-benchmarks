I need to find how many times all three trucks arrive at the warehouse at the same time between 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM.

First, let me establish the arrival patterns:
- Truck A returns every 12 minutes
- Truck B returns every 18 minutes  
- Truck C returns every 30 minutes

All trucks start at 6:00 AM, so I need to find when their schedules align again.

To find when all three trucks arrive simultaneously, I need to find the Least Common Multiple (LCM) of their return intervals.

Finding LCM of 12, 18, and 30:

Prime factorization:
- 12 = 2² × 3
- 18 = 2 × 3²
- 30 = 2 × 3 × 5

LCM = 2² × 3² × 5 = 4 × 9 × 5 = 180 minutes

So all three trucks will arrive together every 180 minutes (3 hours).

Now I need to count how many times this happens between 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM inclusive.

Time period: 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM = 12 hours = 720 minutes

Starting time: 6:00 AM (minute 0)
First arrival together: 6:00 AM (minute 0)
Second arrival together: 6:00 AM + 180 minutes = 9:00 AM
Third arrival together: 9:00 AM + 180 minutes = 12:00 PM
Fourth arrival together: 12:00 PM + 180 minutes = 3:00 PM
Fifth arrival together: 3:00 PM + 180 minutes = 6:00 PM

Let me verify this works:
- At 6:00 AM: All trucks arrive (0 minutes)
- At 9:00 AM: 180 minutes after start → 180 ÷ 12 = 15 (integer), 180 ÷ 18 = 10 (integer), 180 ÷ 30 = 6 (integer) ✓
- At 12:00 PM: 360 minutes after start → 360 ÷ 12 = 30, 360 ÷ 18 = 20, 360 ÷ 30 = 12 ✓
- At 3:00 PM: 540 minutes after start → 540 ÷ 12 = 45, 540 ÷ 18 = 30, 540 ÷ 30 = 18 ✓
- At 6:00 PM: 720 minutes after start → 720 ÷ 12 = 60, 720 ÷ 18 = 40, 720 ÷ 30 = 24 ✓

Since 720 minutes is exactly 4 periods of 180 minutes, the last arrival at 6:00 PM is included.

The arrivals occur at:
- 6:00 AM
- 9:00 AM  
- 12:00 PM
- 3:00 PM
- 6:00 PM

This gives us 5 total times.

ANSWER: 5
