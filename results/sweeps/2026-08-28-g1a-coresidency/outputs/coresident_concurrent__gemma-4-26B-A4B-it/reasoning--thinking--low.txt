To find how many times all three trucks arrive at the warehouse at the same moment between 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM inclusive, we need to determine the interval at which their arrival times coincide. This is found by calculating the Least Common Multiple (LCM) of their individual arrival intervals.

1.  **Identify the arrival intervals:**
    *   Truck A: 12 minutes
    *   Truck B: 18 minutes
    *   Truck C: 30 minutes

2.  **Find the LCM of 12, 18, and 30:**
    *   Prime factorization of 12: $2^2 \times 3^1$
    *   Prime factorization of 18: $2^1 \times 3^2$
    *   Prime factorization of 30: $2^1 \times 3^1 \times 5^1$
    *   To find the LCM, take the highest power of each prime factor present:
        *   $2^2 = 4$
        *   $3^2 = 9$
        *   $5^1 = 5$
    *   $\text{LCM}(12, 18, 30) = 4 \times 9 \times 5 = 180$ minutes.

3.  **Convert the interval to hours:**
    *   $180 \text{ minutes} \div 60 \text{ minutes/hour} = 3 \text{ hours}$.
    The three trucks arrive together every 3 hours.

4.  **Determine the time period:**
    *   The trucks start together at 6:00 AM.
    *   The period is from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM, which is a total of 12 hours.

5.  **List the arrival times:**
