All three are together at a time when that time is a multiple of the least common multiple (LCM) of 12, 18, and 30.

Compute the LCM:
- \(12 = 2^2 \times 3\)
- \(18 = 2 \times 3^2\)
- \(30 = 2 \times 3 \times 5\)

LCM \(= 2^2 \times 3^2 \times 5 = 4 \times 9 \times 5 = 180\) minutes.

Thus, the common arrival times are every 180 minutes after 6:00 AM:
- 0 min = 6:00 AM
- 180 min = 9:00 AM
- 360 min = 12:00 PM
- 540 min = 3:00 PM
- 720 min = 6:00 PM
