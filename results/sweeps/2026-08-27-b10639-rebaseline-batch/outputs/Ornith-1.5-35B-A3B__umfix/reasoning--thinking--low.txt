=== CONTENT ===
## Reasoning

**When do all three trucks coincide?**

They all arrive together at 6:00 AM (t = 0). The next time all three arrive at the same moment is at the least common multiple (LCM) of their return intervals.

Decompose each interval into prime factors:
- 12 = 2² · 3
- 18 = 2 · 3²
- 30 = 2 · 3 · 5

**LCM(12, 18, 30)** = take the highest power of each prime:
$$2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5 = 4 \cdot 9 \cdot 5 = 180 \text{ minutes}$$

So all three trucks arrive together every **180 minutes = 3 hours**.

**Count the coincidences within the window**

The window is 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM inclusive = 12 hours = **720 minutes**.

The trucks meet at times:
- t = 0 min → 6:00 AM
- t = 180 min → 9:00 AM
- t = 360 min → 12:00 PM (noon)
- t = 540 min → 3:00 PM
- t = 720 min → 6:00 PM

All of these fall within (and at the endpoints of) the window. That's the sequence 0, 180, 360, 540, 720.

To verify the count: number of meetings = ⌊720 / 180⌋ + 1 = 4 + 1 = 5 (the +1 includes the starting moment at 6:00 AM).

ANSWER: 5
