=== CONTENT ===
To find how many times all three trucks arrive at the same moment between 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM inclusive, we need to determine the frequency at which their arrival times coincide.

**1. Find the Least Common Multiple (LCM) of the arrival intervals:**
The trucks arrive every 12, 18, and 30 minutes. They will all arrive at the same time at every common multiple of these three numbers. The first time they arrive together after the start is the Least Common Multiple.

*   Prime factorization of 12: $2^2 \times 3$
*   Prime factorization of 18: $2 \times 3^2$
*   Prime factorization of 30: $2 \times 3 \times 5$

The LCM is found by taking the highest power of each prime factor present:
$LCM(12, 18, 30) = 2^2 \times 3^2 \times 5^1$
$LCM = 4 \times 9 \times 5 = 180$ minutes.

**2. Convert the LCM to hours:**
$180 \text{ minutes} = \frac{180}{60} \text{ hours} = 3 \text{ hours}$.
All three trucks arrive together every 3 hours.

**3. Determine the total time period:**
The time from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM is exactly 12 hours.

**4. Calculate the number of occurrences:**
Since the trucks arrive together at the start (6:00 AM) and every 3 hours thereafter, we list the times:
*   6:00 AM (Start)
*   9:00 AM (6:00 AM + 3 hours)
*   12:00 PM (9:00 AM + 3 hours)
*   3:00 PM (12:00 PM + 3 hours)
*   6:00 PM (3:00 PM + 3 hours)

The question asks for the count "between 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM inclusive," so we include both the start and end times.

Counting the occurrences: 6:00 AM, 9:00 AM, 12:00 PM, 3:00 PM, and 6:00 PM gives a total of 5 times.

ANSWER: 5
