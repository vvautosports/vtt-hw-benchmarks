I've implemented the `parse_ranges` function as requested. Here's a summary of what it does:

- Handles empty or whitespace-only strings by returning `[]`
- Parses comma-separated values that can be individual numbers or ranges (e.g., `1-3`)
- Strips whitespace from all parts
- Raises `ValueError` for reversed ranges (like `9-7` or `3-1`)
- Expands ranges to their individual numbers

- Multiple ranges (`1-5,8-10`)
