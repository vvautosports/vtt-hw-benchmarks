=== CONTENT ===
The three trucks start together at 6:00 AM. They will next all arrive together at the least common multiple (LCM) of their return intervals: 12 min, 18 min, and 30 min.

- Prime factorizations:  
  12 = 2² · 3  
  18 = 2 · 3²  
  30 = 2 · 3 · 5  

- LCM = 2² · 3² · 5 = 4 · 9 · 5 = **180 minutes**.

From 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM is 12 hours = 720 minutes. The times (in minutes after 6:00 AM) when all three coincide are the multiples of 180 within \([0, 720]\):

- 0 min (6:00 AM)  
- 180 min (9:00 AM)  
- 360 min (12:00 PM)  
- 540 min (3:00 PM)  
- 720 min (6:00 PM)

That gives 5 occurrences.

ANSWER: 5
