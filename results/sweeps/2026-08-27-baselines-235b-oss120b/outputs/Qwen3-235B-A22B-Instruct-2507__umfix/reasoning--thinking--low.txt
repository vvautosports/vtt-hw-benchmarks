The three delivery trucks return to the warehouse at regular intervals:

- Truck A returns every 12 minutes.
- Truck B returns every 18 minutes.
- Truck C returns every 30 minutes.

We are to determine how many times all three trucks arrive simultaneously between 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM inclusive.

### Step 1: Understand the cycle

Let $ t = 0 $ minutes correspond to 6:00 AM.

Each truck returns at the following intervals:

- Truck A: returns at $ t = 12k $, $ k = 0, 1, 2, \ldots $
- Truck B: returns at $ t = 18m $, $ m = 0, 1, 2, \ldots $
- Truck C: returns at $ t = 30n $, $ n = 0, 1, 2, \ldots $

They all return simultaneously at time $ t $ where:

$$
t \text{ is a multiple of } 12, 18, \text{ and } 30
$$

So the time $ t $ at which all three meet is a common multiple of 12, 18, and 30.

The **least common multiple (LCM)** of these three numbers tells us how often this happens.

### Step 2: Compute LCM of 12, 18, and 30

Factor each number:

- $ 12 = 2^2 \cdot 3 $
- $ 18 = 2 \cdot 3^2 $
- $ 30 = 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 $

To compute the LCM, take the highest power of each prime:

- $ 2^2 $
- $ 3^2 $
- $ 5^1 $

So:

$$
\text{LCM} = 2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5 = 4 \cdot 9 \cdot 5 = 36 \cdot 5 = 180
$$

So the three trucks return together every **180 minutes**.

### Step 3: Determine time window

We are interested in times from **6:00 AM** to **6:00 PM** inclusive.

This is a **12-hour** window.

Convert to minutes:

$$
12 \text{ hours} \times 60 = 720 \text{ minutes}
$$

So we consider $ t = 0 $ (6:00 AM) up to and including $ t = 720 $ (6:00 PM).

The trucks meet simultaneously every 180 minutes, starting at $ t = 0 $.

So the times they meet are:

$$
t = 0, 180, 360, 540, 720
$$

### Step 4: Count how many times

- $ t = 0 $: 6:00 AM
- $ t = 180 $: 9:00 AM
- $ t = 360 $: 12:00 PM
- $ t = 540 $: 3:00 PM
- $ t = 720 $: 6:00 PM

That is **5** times.

And note that 720 is a multiple of 180:

$$
720 \div 180 = 4 \Rightarrow t = 4 \times 180 = 720
$$

So yes, they meet at both 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM.

### Final Answer

ANSWER: 5
