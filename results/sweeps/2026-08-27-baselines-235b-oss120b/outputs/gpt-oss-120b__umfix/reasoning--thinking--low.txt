=== REASONING ===
LCM 180 minutes = 3 hours. Starting at 6:00, they coincide at multiples of 180 minutes up to 12 hours (720 minutes). Count inclusive of start and end? They arrive together at 6:00 AM initial. Also at 6:00 PM? 6:00 PM is 12 hours later = 720 minutes, which is multiple of 180 (720/180=4). So times: 0,180,360,540,720 => 5 times.

=== CONTENT ===
The trucks meet whenever the elapsed time since 6:00 AM is a common multiple of their return intervals.  
- Truck A: every 12 min  
- Truck B: every 18 min  
- Truck C: every 30 min  

The least common multiple of 12, 18, 30 is  

\[
\text{lcm}(12,18,30)=180\text{ minutes}=3\text{ hours}.
\]

Thus the trucks all arrive together at 6:00 AM and then every 180 minutes thereafter.  

From 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM inclusive is a 12‑hour span = 720 minutes.  
The multiples of 180 minutes within this interval are:

\[
0,\;180,\;360,\;540,\;720\text{ minutes}.
\]

That gives **5** simultaneous arrivals (including the start and the end).

**ANSWER: 5**
