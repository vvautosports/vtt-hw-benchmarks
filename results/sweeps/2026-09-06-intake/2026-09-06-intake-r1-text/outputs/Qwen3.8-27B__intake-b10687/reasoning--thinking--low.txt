=== REASONING ===
We need to find how many times all three trucks arrive at the same moment between 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM inclusive.

Truck A returns every 12 minutes
Truck B returns every 18 minutes
Truck C returns every 30 minutes

They all start together at 6:00 AM. They will all be at the warehouse simultaneously at times that are multiples of the LCM of 12, 18, and 30.

Let me find LCM(12, 18, 30):
- 12 = 2² × 3
- 18 = 2 × 3²
- 30 = 2 × 3 × 5

LCM = 2² × 3² × 5 = 4 × 9 × 5 = 180 minutes

So they all arrive together every 180 minutes.

Now, the time from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM is 12 hours = 720 minutes.

The times when all three arrive together (measured from 6:00 AM) are:
0, 180, 360, 540, 720 minutes

That's 5 times.

Let me verify:
- 0 min = 6:00 AM ✓
- 180 min = 9:00 AM ✓
- 360 min = 12:00 PM ✓
- 540 min = 3:00 PM ✓
- 720 min = 6:00 PM ✓

So the answer is 5.


=== CONTENT ===
## Finding When All Three Trucks Arrive Together

**Step 1: Find the LCM of the return intervals.**

- Truck A: every 12 minutes → 12 = 2² × 3
- Truck B: every 18 minutes → 18 = 2 × 3²
- Truck C: every 30 minutes → 30 = 2 × 3 × 5

$$\text{LCM} = 2^2 \times 3^2 \times 5 = 180 \text{ minutes}$$

All three trucks arrive together every **180 minutes**.

**Step 2: Determine the time window.**

From 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM is 12 hours = **720 minutes**.

**Step 3: Count the simultaneous arrivals.**

The trucks meet at times (measured from 6:00 AM):

| Minutes from 6 AM | Clock Time |
|---|---|
| 0 | 6:00 AM |
| 180 | 9:00 AM |
| 360 | 12:00 PM |
| 540 | 3:00 PM |
| 720 | 6:00 PM |

That gives us $\frac{720}{180} + 1 = 5$ simultaneous arrivals (including both endpoints).

ANSWER: 5
