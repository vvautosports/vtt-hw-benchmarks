## Setting Up the Problem

All three trucks arrive together at times that are **common multiples** of their individual intervals (12, 18, and 30 minutes). I need to find when these coincide.

## Finding the Least Common Multiple

**Prime factorizations:**
- 12 = 2² × 3
- 18 = 2 × 3²
- 30 = 2 × 3 × 5

**LCM** = take the highest power of each prime:
$$\text{LCM} = 2^2 \times 3^2 \times 5 = 4 \times 9 \times 5 = 180 \text{ minutes}$$

So all three trucks arrive together **every 180 minutes (3 hours)**.

## Counting the Coarrivals

The total time window from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM is **12 hours = 720 minutes**.

The trucks coincide at every multiple of 180 minutes:

| Time | Minutes since 6:00 AM |
|------|----------------------|
| 6:00 AM | 0 |
| 9:00 AM | 180 |
| 12:00 PM | 360 |
| 3:00 PM | 540 |
| 6:00 PM | 720 |

Since 720 ÷ 180 = 4 exactly, the 6:00 PM arrival is included (the window is inclusive).

Counting these: 6:00 AM, 9:00 AM, noon, 3:00 PM, and 6:00 PM — a total of 5 coincidences.

ANSWER: 5
