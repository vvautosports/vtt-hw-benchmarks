=== CONTENT ===
**Reasoning:**

The trucks coincide whenever the elapsed time is a common multiple of 12, 18, and 30.

- Prime factorizations: 12 = 2²·3, 18 = 2·3², 30 = 2·3·5
- LCM = 2²·3²·5 = **180 minutes = 3 hours**

So all three arrive together every 3 hours. The window from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM is 12 hours = 720 minutes.

Coincident arrivals occur at minute 0, 180, 360, 540, 720 — i.e., 6:00 AM, 9:00 AM, 12:00 PM, 3:00 PM, and 6:00 PM. Since both endpoints are included in the count, that's 720/180 + 1 = 5 moments (verified by direct enumeration above).

ANSWER: 5
