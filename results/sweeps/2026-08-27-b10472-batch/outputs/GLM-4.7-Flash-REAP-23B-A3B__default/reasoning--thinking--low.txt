The problem is asking: Three delivery trucks all arrive at a warehouse together at 6:00 AM. Truck A returns every 12 minutes, truck B every 15 minutes, and truck C every 20 minutes. Between 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM inclusive, how many times do all three trucks arrive at the same moment?

We need to find the least common multiple of the three intervals (12, 15, 20 minutes). Then find how many multiples of that LCM occur within the time interval from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM inclusive.

Let me compute the LCM of 12, 15, and 20.

First, find prime factorizations:
- 12 = 2^2 * 3
- 15 = 3 * 5
- 20 = 2^2 * 5

LCM takes the highest power of each prime:
- For 2: max(2^2) = 2^2
- For 3: max(3^1) = 3^1
- For 3 (also from 15): done
- For 5: max(5^1) = 5^1

So LCM = 2^2 * 3 * 5 = 4 * 3 * 5 = 60 minutes.

Thus, all three trucks arrive together every 60 minutes.

The total number of hourly coincidences in this interval is:

\frac{720\text{ min}}{60\text{ min}} + 1 = 12 + 1 = 13
