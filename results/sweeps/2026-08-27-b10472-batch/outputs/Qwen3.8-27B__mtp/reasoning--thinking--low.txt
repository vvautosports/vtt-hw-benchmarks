**Reasoning:**

All three trucks arrive simultaneously whenever the elapsed time from 6:00 AM is a common multiple of their return intervals (12, 18, and 30 minutes). The least common multiple is:

- 12 = 2² · 3
- 18 = 2 · 3²
- 30 = 2 · 3 · 5
- LCM = 2² · 3² · 5 = **180 minutes (3 hours)**

So they coincide every 3 hours. The window from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM spans 720 minutes, and counting the endpoints gives 720 ÷ 180 + 1 = 5 arrivals:

- 6:00 AM
- 9:00 AM
- 12:00 PM
- 3:00 PM
- 6:00 PM

ANSWER: 5
