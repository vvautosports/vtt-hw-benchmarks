The three trucks will all be at the warehouse together whenever a time is a common multiple of their return intervals: 12 min, 18 min, and 30 min.

1. Find the least common multiple (LCM):
   - Prime factorization  
     - 12 = \(2^2 \times 3\)  
     - 18 = \(2 \times 3^2\)  
     - 30 = \(2 \times 3 \times 5\)  
   - Take the highest power of each prime: \(2^2\), \(3^2\), \(5\).  
   - LCM = \(2^2 \times 3^2 \times 5 = 4 \times 9 \times 5 = 180\) minutes.

2. Starting at 6:00 AM (time = 0 min), the simultaneous arrivals occur every 180 minutes:
   - 0 min → 6:00 AM  
   - 180 min → 9:00 AM  
   - 360 min → 12:00 PM  
   - 540 min → 3:00 PM  
   - 720 min → 6:00 PM  

3. The interval from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM inclusive is 720 minutes, so we include both the start (0 min) and the endpoint (720 min). That gives **5** moments when all three trucks arrive together.
