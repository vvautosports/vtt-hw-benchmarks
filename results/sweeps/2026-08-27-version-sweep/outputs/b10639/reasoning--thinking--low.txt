=== REASONING ===
We have a problem about three delivery trucks arriving at a warehouse on a fixed schedule:
- Truck A returns every 12 minutes
- Truck B returns every 18 minutes
- Truck C returns every 30 minutes

All three trucks arrive together at 6:00 AM. We need to determine how many times, between 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM inclusive (i.e., from 6:00 to 18:00 on the same day), they all arrive at the same moment.

This is a classic "LCM problem". The times when all three arrive together are at intervals equal to the least common multiple (LCM) of their individual intervals.

First, find the LCM of 12, 18, and 30.

Let's compute:
- Prime factors:
  - 12 = 2^2 × 3^1
  - 18 = 2^1 × 3^2
  - 30 = 2^1 × 3^1 × 5^1
- LCM takes the highest power of each prime: 2^2, 3^2, 5^1
  - LCM = 4 × 9 × 5 = 180 minutes = 3 hours.

So the three trucks will all arrive together every 180 minutes (3 hours).

Now, we count the number of times they all arrive together from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM inclusive.

The times (in minutes after 6:00 AM) when they coincide:
- 0 (6:00 AM)
- 180 (9:00 AM)
- 360 (12:00 PM/Noon)
- 540 (3:00 PM)
- 720 (6:0 PM)

Let's verify: 720 minutes = 12 hours after 6:00 AM is 6:00 PM. So the list includes 0, 180, 360, 540, 720 minutes.

Thus there are 5 times when they all arrive at the same moment.

Answer is 5.

Let's double-check by enumerating the times: At 6:00, 9:00, 12:00, 3:00, 6:00 PM.

Alternatively, we can solve: The total time interval is 12 hours = 12*60 = 720 minutes. The interval between coincidences is 180 minutes. The number of multiples of 180 within [0, 720] inclusive is floor(720/180) + 1 = 4 + 1 = 5.

Thus answer: 5.

=== CONTENT ===
The three trucks arrive together when the amount of time since 6:00 AM is a common multiple of 12, 18, and 30 minutes.  
The smallest such interval is the **least common multiple** (LCM).

**Find the LCM of 12, 18, and 30:**
- 12 = 2² × 3  
- 18 = 2 × 3²  
- 30 = 2 × 3 × 5  

Take the highest power of each prime:
- 2 → 2² (from 12)
- 3 → 3² (from 18)
- 5 → 5¹ (from 30)

LCM = 2² × 3² × 5 = 4 × 9 × 5 = **180 minutes = 3 hours**.

Thus all three trucks meet every 180 minutes.

**Count the meetings from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM inclusive:**
- Total time span = 12 hours = 12 × 60 = **720 minutes**.
- Number of 180‑minute intervals in 720 minutes = 720 ÷ 180 = 4.
- Including the starting moment at 6:00 AM, we have 4 + 1 = **5 meetings**.

The meeting times are:
- 6:00 AM (0 min)
- 9:00 AM (180 min)
- 12:00 PM (360 min)
- 3:00 PM (540 min)
- 6:00 PM (720 min)

**ANSWER: 5**
