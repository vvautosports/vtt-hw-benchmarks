The least common multiple (LCM) of these intervals is:

- 12 = 2² · 3
- 18 = 2 · 3²
- 30 = 2 · 3 · 5  

LCM = 2² · 3² · 5 = 4 · 9 · 5 = 180 minutes (3 hours).
